Our Promise

Handcrafted in the heart of the orchard

Happy Bear Cider began as one man's homebrew obsession and grew into something the whole creek could celebrate. Every bottle carries the flavor of a specific place — the ridge, the barrel house, the berry bluff — and the spirit of a bear who never rushes a good thing.

We press in Pennsylvania. We ferment slow. We don't cut corners and we don't apologize for being exactly what we are: a cozy, handcrafted cider for people who like their drinks the way they like their Saturdays — unhurried.
