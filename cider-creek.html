The Regions

The Lay of the Land

Cider Creek isn't just a name — it's a place. Each region has its own terrain, its own character, and its own cider that couldn't have come from anywhere else.

🍎

Orchard Ridge

The high ground · Where it all begins

Where the apple trees grow oldest and tallest. On clear mornings, you can see three counties from the top of the ridge. The air is crisp and bright, and the apples up here take longer to ripen — which is exactly why they're worth waiting for.

Heritage varieties mingle with newer plantings. Each fall, the harvest from Orchard Ridge becomes the backbone of the whole lineup.

Home of: The Happy Pour
🛢️

Barrel Hill

The hollow · Where patience lives

Half a mile down from the ridge, the trail curves into a hollow where the air smells of oak and time. The barrel house sits here — low, wide, and always slightly warm, no matter the season outside.

This is where Autumn Hug ages, slowly taking on the character of the wood. Nobody rushes anything on Barrel Hill. That would be missing the whole point.

Home of: Autumn Hug
🫐

Berry Patch Bluff

The wild side · Where the fun is

Follow the creek east until the trail goes purple. Berry Patch Bluff is wild in the best way — overgrown with blueberry brambles, blackberries, and wildflowers that nobody planted and nobody minds.

Cider Creek Blue starts here, with buckets of hand-picked berries and a bear who comes back every year with very stained paws and a very satisfied expression.

Home of: Cider Creek Blue
