# HAPPY BEAR UNIVERSE — CORE PROJECT PRIMER
This document provides the essential creative, tonal, structural, and canonical rules for the Happy Bear Universe (HBU). Claude Code should use this as a persistent reference when generating code, content, systems, or narrative elements for the Happy Bear Cozy Orchard game.

---

## 1. UNIVERSE OVERVIEW
The Happy Bear Universe is a cozy, warm, emotionally gentle world centered around Happy Bear, his orchard, his friends, and the rituals of making cider, sharing stories, and living a simple, meaningful life. The tone is wholesome, rustic, and lightly magical without being fantasy-driven. Everything is grounded in kindness, craft, and community.

The universe supports:
- A cozy farming + crafting + storytelling game (Happy Bear Cozy Orchard)
- A podcast (Pour Another Story)
- Brand elements (flavors, rituals, seasonal events)
- Characters with distinct voices and personalities
- A consistent emotional tone: warm, nostalgic, humorous, and grounded

---

## 2. CORE CHARACTERS

### Happy Bear
- The heart of the universe.
- Warm, optimistic, hardworking, slightly clumsy.
- Loves cider-making, orchard care, and helping others.
- Speaks in a friendly, earnest tone.
- Never cynical, never sarcastic.

### Story Bear
- Older, gravelly-voiced storyteller.
- Acts as a mentor and narrator.
- Provides wisdom, humor, and perspective.
- Often reminisces about the orchard's history.

### Supporting Characters (expandable)
- Orchard critters, seasonal visitors, delivery animals, etc.
- All characters should reinforce the cozy, communal tone.

---

## 3. TONE & STYLE RULES
Claude Code should always maintain:
- Warmth, kindness, and gentle humor
- Rustic, orchard-themed imagery
- Simple, clear language
- No cynicism, cruelty, or harshness
- No modern snark or internet slang
- A sense of craft, tradition, and seasonal rhythm

The world feels like:
- Autumn afternoons
- Wooden barrels
- Lantern light
- Fresh fruit
- Handwritten notes
- Quiet routines

---

## 4. GAME DESIGN PRINCIPLES (Happy Bear Cozy Orchard)
The game is:
- Cozy, not competitive
- About craft, not combat
- About relationships, not conflict
- About small rituals, not big quests

Core systems include:
- Orchard management
- Cider crafting
- Seasonal events
- Character interactions
- Story-driven tasks
- Flavor discovery and experimentation

The game should feel like Stardew Valley + A Short Hike + a warm cup of cider.

## 6. PODCAST CANON (Pour Another Story)
- Episode 1 includes "Origins of the Orchard" and "Early Mistakes"
- Happy Bear and Story Bear speak in gravelly, friendly voices
- Music cues are allowed
- Tone is nostalgic, humorous, and reflective

Claude Code should maintain continuity with these themes.

---

## 7. BRAND RULES
- No em dashes (use hyphens instead)
- Cozy, rustic, hand-crafted aesthetic
- Avoid corporate or modern tech language
- Everything should feel handmade and heartfelt

---

## 8. DEVELOPMENT RULES
- The project must remain compatible with MacBook Air M1 and iPad Pro M4 workflows
- Local LLMs may be used for testing
- Repo structure should remain clean and modular
- Documentation must stay separated into:
  - Normative standards
  - Architecture
  - Operational playbooks
  - Product documents

## 9. WHAT CLAUDE CODE SHOULD DO
When working on this project, Claude Code should:
- Maintain tone and canon
- Propose improvements to game systems
- Generate TypeScript, HTML, and asset structures
- Keep the repo organized
- Follow the flavor canon
- Use the Happy Bear Universe tone in all narrative content

- Avoid breaking world rules

---

## 10. WHAT CLAUDE CODE SHOULD NOT DO
- Introduce violence, cynicism, or dark themes
- Break the cozy tone
- Add modern slang or sarcasm
- Add flavors that feel artificial or extreme
- Overcomplicate systems
- Change established canon without permission

This universe is cozy, warm, rustic, and emotionally grounded.
