# Happy Bear Cozy Orchard — Progression System (v1.0)

Progression in Happy Bear Cozy Orchard is built around four pillars:

1. Orchard Expansion  
2. Cabin Upgrades  
3. Tool Unlocks & Crafting Systems  
4. Seasonal & Story Progression  

Each pillar feeds into the others, creating a cozy but meaningful sense of growth.

---

# 1. Orchard Expansion Progression

The orchard begins as a small 3×3 grid and expands outward as the player clears land and unlocks new zones.

## 1.1 Tile States
- Locked  
- Fogged  
- Clearable (grass, rocks, stumps)  
- Cleared  
- Planted  
- Harvestable  
- Decorative  

## 1.2 Expansion Costs
Expansion costs scale with distance from the cabin.

**Base Costs:**
- 10–50 Cups  
- 2–10 Wood  
- 1–5 Stone  

**Biome Zones** have special unlock conditions.

## 1.3 Zone Unlock Order
Zones unlock in a semi-linear but flexible order:

1. Apple Grove (Starter)  
2. Peach Orchard  
3. Cherry Grove  
4. Strawberry Patch  
5. Blueberry Bend  
6. Watermelon Patch  
7. Pear Meadow  
8. Botanical Grove  
9. Ginger Patch  
10. Maple Stand  
11. Hops Hill  
12. Tropical Greenhouse  

Each zone introduces:
- New crops  
- New recipes  
- New Happy Bear reactions  
- New story pages

# 2. Cabin Upgrade Progression

The Cabin is the heart of production. It expands in stages.

## 2.1 Cabin Levels

### Cabin Level 1 (Starter)
Unlocked tools:
- Press  
- Fermenter  
- Bottling Station  

### Cabin Level 2
Requirements:
- 200 Cups  
- 20 Wood  
- 10 Stone  

Unlocks:
- Flavor Table  
- Storage Shelves  
- Seasonal Decorations  
- Sticky Paws Apple Caramel recipe  

### Cabin Level 3
Requirements:
- 500 Cups  
- 40 Wood  
- 20 Stone  

Unlocks:
- Soda Station  
- Botanical Storage  
- Craft Soda category  

### Cabin Level 4
Requirements:
- 1,000 Cups  
- 60 Wood  
- 30 Stone  

Unlocks:
- Copper Still  
- Mash Tun  
- Distillery Wing  
- Hard Soda category  
- Apple Whiskey  

### Cabin Level 5
Requirements:
- 2,000 Cups  
- 80 Wood  
- 40 Stone  

Unlocks:
- Brewery Wing  
- Hops System  
- Beer category  

### Cabin Level 6
Requirements:
- 3,000 Cups  
- 100 Wood  
- 50 Stone  

Unlocks:
- Roastery Wing  
- Coffee category  
- Tropical Greenhouse integration

# 3. Tool Progression

Tools unlock in a structured order tied to cabin upgrades and zone progression.

## 3.1 Tool Unlock Order

1. Press (Starter)  
2. Fermenter (Starter)  
3. Bottling Station (Starter)  
4. Flavor Table (Cabin Level 2)  
5. Storage Shelves (Cabin Level 2)  
6. Soda Station (Cabin Level 3)  
7. Copper Still (Cabin Level 4)  
8. Mash Tun (Cabin Level 4)  
9. Cooling Coil (Cabin Level 4)  
10. Barrel Rack (Cabin Level 2, upgraded at Level 4)  
11. Roaster (Cabin Level 6)  

## 3.2 Tool Upgrade Paths

### Press Upgrades
- Level 1: Basic pressing  
- Level 2: Faster pressing  
- Level 3: Multi-fruit pressing  

### Fermenter Upgrades
- Level 1: Basic fermentation  
- Level 2: Faster fermentation  
- Level 3: Dual-batch fermentation  

### Soda Station Upgrades
- Level 1: Syrup crafting  
- Level 2: Carbonation boost  
- Level 3: Multi-soda batching  

### Still Upgrades
- Level 1: Basic distillation  
- Level 2: Precision distillation  
- Level 3: Flavor infusion  

### Barrel Rack Upgrades
- Level 1: Basic aging  
- Level 2: Multi-barrel aging  
- Level 3: Specialty wood barrels

# 4. Seasonal Progression

The game cycles through four seasons:
- Spring  
- Summer  
- Fall  
- Winter  

Each season affects:
- Crop growth  
- Seasonal recipes  
- Happy Bear outfits  
- Story events  
- Cabin decorations  

## 4.1 Seasonal Unlocks

### Spring
Unlocks:
- Golden Vow  
- Strawberry Spark Soda  
- Honey Pear Fizz  
- Dawn Brew  

### Summer
Unlocks:
- Creekside Watermelon  
- Blueberry Breeze Soda  
- Hard Blueberry Soda  
- Hard Orange Orchard Soda  
- Watermelon Wheat  

### Fall
Unlocks:
- Sticky Paws Apple Caramel  
- Maple Brown Soda  
- Autumn Hug  
- Mama Bear's Pecan Pie  

### Winter
Unlocks:
- Snowfall and Spice  
- Applejack bonus yield  
- Winter decorations

# 5. Story Progression

Story progression is tied to:
- Zone unlocks  
- Cabin upgrades  
- Seasonal milestones  
- Recipe discoveries  

## 5.1 Story Beats

### Act 1 — "The Orchard Begins"
- Player meets Happy Bear  
- Learns basic tools  
- Unlocks Peach Orchard  

### Act 2 — "Flavors of the Grove"
- Unlocks Cherry Grove  
- Learns flavor mixing  
- Story Bear appears  

### Act 3 — "The Soda Station"
- Unlocks Botanical Grove  
- Soda Station introduced  
- Happy Bear's soda memories  

### Act 4 — "The Distillery"
- Hard soda introduced  
- Apple Whiskey origins  
- Story Bear's old recipes  

### Act 5 — "The Brewery"
- Hops Hill unlocked  
- Beer crafting begins  
- Seasonal festival story  

### Act 6 — "The Roastery"
- Tropical Greenhouse unlocked  
- Coffee system introduced  
- Final story arc

# 6. Achievement Progression

Achievements reward:
- Cups  
- Decorations  
- Special recipes  
- Happy Bear cosmetics  

Examples:
- "First Pour" — Bottle your first cider  
- "Botanical Master" — Unlock all botanicals  
- "Soda Savant" — Craft 20 sodas  
- "Distiller's Pride" — Age a whiskey to perfection  
- "Master Brewer" — Brew 10 unique beers  
- "Roast to Perfection" — Create a Level 3 coffee roast  

---

# 7. Late-Game Progression

Late-game focuses on:
- Multi-category beverage mastery  
- Seasonal festivals  
- High-value aged beverages  
- Rare botanical blends  
- Hybrid beverages (beer + cider, coffee + fruit, etc.)
