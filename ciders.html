The Lineup

Our Ciders

From the crisp heart of Orchard Ridge to the warm depths of Barrel Hill — every cider tells a story from the creek.

Always Available

Core Ciders

These three are the backbone of Happy Bear — year-round, always fresh, always right.

🍎
Core

The Happy Pour

Classic dry apple · Orchard Ridge

The one that started it all. Crisp, clean, and confidently dry — pressed from a blend of heritage orchard apples and fermented slow over weeks. Happy Bear at its most essential. A benchmark cider that earns its place on every table.

Fresh-cut apple · dry finish · light floral · crisp carbonation
🍂
Core

Autumn Hug

Spiced apple · Barrel Hill

Aged in oak barrels on Barrel Hill until it finds that perfect balance of spice and warmth. Autumn Hug tastes like the first cold morning of fall — a cinnamon-kissed, full-bodied embrace you'll reach for all season long.

Cinnamon · toasted oak · warm apple · vanilla undertone
🫐
Core

Cider Creek Blue

Wild blueberry · Berry Patch Bluff

From the blueberry brambles of Berry Patch Bluff comes this bright, berry-forward cider. Lightly tart and beautifully hued, Cider Creek Blue is summer in a bottle — wild, sweet, and unapologetically fun.

Fresh blueberry · bright tartness · light sweetness · clean finish
Limited & Seasonal

Special Releases

When the season calls, the bear answers. These ciders arrive when they're ready — and leave when they're gone.

🍺
Seasonal

The Long Pour

Extended ferment · Orchard Reserve

Some things can't be rushed. The Long Pour is slow-fermented for an extended period, developing a complex, layered depth that rewards patience. A sipper's cider — made for long evenings and good company.

Complex apple · subtle tannins · dry minerality
🌿
Seasonal

Washington's Orchard

Heritage apple blend · Historical Series

A tribute to American orchard heritage. This cider blends traditional American apple varieties into something that feels both old-world and distinctly of this place. Dry, grounded, and full of character.

Heritage apple · dry finish · earthy undertones
✨
Limited

Golden Vow

Honey apple · Celebration Series

Crafted for the moments that matter. Golden Vow blends apple cider with local wildflower honey for a semi-sweet, celebratory pour. Reserve it for the toasts worth remembering.

Wildflower honey · golden apple · soft sweetness · elegant finish
🍎
Fall Seasonal

Sticky Paws Apple Caramel

Caramel apple · Fall Release

Happy Bear's most playful cider. Sticky Paws leans all the way into caramel apple season — dessert-sweet, fall-flavored, and dangerously drinkable. Best enjoyed with a bonfire and a flannel shirt.

Caramel · baked apple · brown sugar · warm spice
❄️
Winter Seasonal

Snowfall and Spice

Winter spiced apple · Holiday Release

The creek gets quiet when the snow falls. Snowfall and Spice captures that stillness — a warmly spiced cider perfect for cold nights, hot mugs, and the kind of cozy that only winter brings.

Mulling spices · apple · clove · gentle warmth
🥧
Limited

Mama Bear's Famous Pecan Pie

Pecan · Brown sugar · Holiday Special

Every year, Mama Bear's recipe makes one final appearance. Rich with toasted pecan, brown sugar, and a buttery finish — this limited holiday release sells out fast. Don't say we didn't warn you.

Toasted pecan · brown sugar · buttery apple · rich finish
🌿
Non-Alcoholic

Happy Bear Root Beer

Craft root beer · All ages

A love letter to the classic root beer float — but grown up. Brewed with real sassafras and vanilla, Happy Bear Root Beer is for the cub at the table who's not quite 21 yet. Or for anyone who just wants something delicious.

Sassafras · vanilla cream · classic root beer
🔥
Reserve

Campfire Reserve

Smoked apple · Barrel Reserve

The rarest pour in the creek. Campfire Reserve is a smoked, barrel-aged cider made in tiny batches and released only when the timing is exactly right. Dark, complex, and unforgettable.

Smoked apple · char · dark fruit · long finish
