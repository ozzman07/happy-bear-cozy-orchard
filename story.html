The Backstory

Our Story

A homebrew kit, a backyard orchard, and a bear who knew something the rest of us didn't.

How It Began

Origins of the Orchard

In 2015, while everyone else was chasing craft beer, one Pennsylvania home brewer decided to try something different. He had apples. He had a homebrew kit. He had more time than sense and just enough stubbornness to see it through.

The first batch was rough — too sweet, too flat, not quite right. But there was something there. A warmth in the flavor, a sense of place that beer never quite captured. He kept going.

“The orchard doesn't rush. Neither should the cider.”

By 2017, the batches had names. By 2018, friends were refusing to leave without a growler. By 2020, what started in a garage had a label, a logo, and a bear with a very big smile.

The Legend

Meet Happy Bear

The name Asbjorn is Old Norse — it means “divine bear.” It's also the root of Osborn, the family name that got anglicized somewhere along the way across the Atlantic. One letter dropped, one ocean crossed, one bear transformed into a man. Or maybe the other way around.

Happy Bear started as a joke — a cartoon scrawled on a napkin to explain why the homebrew smelled faintly of the woods. But the bear stuck. He was cheerful without being corny. Cozy without being cloying. He belonged in an orchard, and he looked like he'd been there forever.

Asbjorn became Happy Bear. The napkin became a label. And the label became a brand that tastes exactly like where it came from: warm, unhurried, and a little bit wild.

“The bear was right about the cider all along.”
